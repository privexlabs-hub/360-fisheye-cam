About these effects

Tiny planet cuts you out of the camera picture and places you, through a fisheye lens, in front of a tiny-planet scene. The built-in scene is an illustration; choose My planet image to use your own picture (a 2:1 360° panorama gives a true tiny planet; other photos are mirrored to close the circle).

Real background · planet curls your real surroundings into a planet. A normal camera only sees what is in front of it, so the area outside its view is filled by mirroring and blending the edges of the picture. It is a 360-style effect, not a 360° capture.

Real background · fisheye is an extreme fisheye lens with a face pop that follows your face.

Tips: hold the phone at arm's length a little above eye level and tilt it down. Good light helps face tracking and the cut-out.

All processing happens on this device. Face tracking and the cut-out use Google MediaPipe models that are stored with this app.
